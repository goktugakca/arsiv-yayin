Arşiv Yayın Planlayıcı

Personal upload scheduler

Uses YouTube API Services. This tool uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.

Arşiv Yayın Planlayıcı is a private command-line tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and publishes the short videos the owner makes to the owner's own channels, at the times the owner chooses. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up for it, sign in to it or use it.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to the owner's YouTube channel as private, with a scheduled publish time.
  • Adds the video to one of the owner's own playlists.
  • After the video goes public, checks its status and posts one comment from the channel.
  • Publishes the same video as a Reel on the owner's Instagram account.

Channels it publishes to

Only channels owned and run by the same person:

  • Esrarengiz Arşiv: @esrarengizarsiv
  • Şüpheli Tahtası: @suphelitahtasi
  • Şeffaf Arşiv

Each channel is connected through Google's own OAuth consent screen, by the owner, one channel at a time.
